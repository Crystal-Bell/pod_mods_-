1. Concept Viability: The "Oil Sniffer" & Collection Dynamics
Is centralized collection harmful?
 * Concentration Risk: Gathering uncontained, volatile hydrocarbons into a single dense mass without active stabilization creates explosive vapor zones and concentrated toxicity hot spots.
 * The Remediation Fix: Instead of dragging bulk raw crude into one massive open pool, the brigade must use in-situ sorption, micro-phase separation, and containerized skimming. You collect continuously, but you store and segregate in modular, sealed transport bladders on the water surface.
2. Autonomous Oil Collection: "Raspberry Pod Brigade"
We can structure this into a dedicated category of low-cost, decentralized surface and subsurface remediation bots built on modular single-board compute (Raspberry Pi/ESP32 sensor clusters).
Swarm Architecture & Bot Taxonomy
 * Alpha Unit: The Hydrocarbon Sniffer Node
   * Function: Autonomous surface drone equipped with fluorometric and optical hydrocarbon sensors.
   * Role: Patrols maritime perimeters, maps VOC (volatile organic compound) plumes, and broadcasts real-time telemetry to the collection units.
 * Beta Unit: The Skimmer & Phase Separator (Collector Bot)
   * Function: Oleophilic (oil-attracting/water-repelling) rotating drum skimmer powered by low-draw solar/battery banks.
   * Role: Pulls oil directly off the surface layer, passes it through a centripetal phase separator, and pumps clean water out while transferring concentrated oil into towed bio-degradable bladder modules.
 * Gamma Unit: Bio-Augmentation & Micro-Dispersion Node
   * Function: Secondary response drone loaded with organic hydrocarbon-digesting microbes and bio-surfactants.
   * Role: Deploys targeted bio-remediation agents exclusively around thin sheen edges where mechanical skimming hits diminishing returns.
3. Legacy Boat & Ship Containment: The "Bilge-Guard" Universal Pod
To address legacy boats, commercial hulls, and derelict vessels before they turn into localized disasters, we define a standardized, self-contained single unit: The Sentinel Leak & Bilge Pod.
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                   THE SENTINEL BILGE & HULL POD                   |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  [Sensory Array]  -> Hydrocarbon Sniffer + Hydrostatic Pressure  |
|  [Separation]     -> Multi-Stage Oleophilic Filter / Coalescer   |
|  [Fluid Route]    -> Clean Bilge Water OUT | Oil to Sealed Cell |
|  [Hull Patching]  -> Magnetic Hydro-Gel Seal Deployment Arm      |
|  [Telemetry]      -> Cellular/LoRa Mesh Siren & Leak Alert      |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+

Unit Specifications
 * Multi-Fluid Detection & Auto-Sorting:
   * Uses optical refractive sensors inside the bilge sump to instantly differentiate between clean seawater/greywater, diesel, heavy fuel oil, and hydraulic fluid.
 * Selective Pumping Engine:
   * Never pumps raw oily water overboard. Clean water is discharged through an inline oleophilic absorption matrix (keeping discharge under 15 ppm). Oil and fuel are routed into an onboard flexible holding bladder.
 * Active Hull Breach Patching (Adaptive Cushion):
   * Equipped with a passive magnetic hydro-gel patch kit that can be manually or automatically positioned over seam leaks or weeping rivets on steel and aluminum hulls to halt ingress instantly.
 * Standalone Telemetry Node:
   * Battery-backed mesh radio broadcasts continuous bilge health metrics, pump cycles, and fluid contamination levels to nearby monitoring stations or local vessel owners.
